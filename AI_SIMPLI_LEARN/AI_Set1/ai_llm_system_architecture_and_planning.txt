RAG vs Fine Tuning
rag looks up your doc at ans time, FT retrains the model on your example
rag example open-book exam, FT study for closed-book exam
rag just update the doc, FT retrains the model again
rag shows its sources, it can point, FT, no knowledge is blended in
rag cost is lower and is quicker, FT is costly and is slow
rag is best for facts policies, prices and products, FT is for tone, behaviour and format

what gives better result

    worked for both familiar and brand new facts

RAG Architecture - 2 parts
1 ingestion - done in advance, and store docs for searching
2 querying  - for every question, find the right chunk, then answer

vector db:
    stores meaning as numbers (embeddings model is trained responsible for words into numbers sets, tripplets, +ve, -ve, neutral)
    similar ideas sit together
    matches by meaning, not exact words
    examples pinecone, chroma, FAISS


ingestion process: collection info->clean->cut into small chunks->turn chunks into numbers->store in vector db
querying(answer a question) - ask question->turned into numbers->vector db finds closest chunks->best match is picked->matches are added to the prompt->AI writes the answer with sources
k-experimental value, keep tweaking to get an optimal number for better outcome
