reactive ai answers when you ask, an agentic ai takes steps on its own to reach a goal

why users stop trusting agents
when an agent acts on its own, three worries appear. each one has design fix
i cant stop it, fix clear undo and override buttons--big red signals
why did it do that, fix a short simple reason - red signals
is it even true, fix show its confidence - red signals
always study what agent is doing and generating
thinking........ expand arrows, see what its thinking, planning, running commands and always weighs test manually what it has been generating
for any ai solutions, provide a confidence meter so trust on ai solutions is maintained

six principals of agentic ux
1 clearity - plain words ie your flight leaves at 6:10am
2 transparency - say why it chose something and how sure it is, eg gemini always gives summary of what is doing, whichever links is souring from, it shows
3 predictablity - same pattern every time, so users know what comes next. eg red light always means stop, green is go
4 user control - easy undo, edit or confirm, especially before anything import
5 fail safe design - when unsure of failing, fall back safely, ask a person or do nothing
6 feedback loop - learn from thumbs up/down and from every correction. always remember how convinient you can make for the user will use your ai solution. It needs to optional but always design feedback loop
the hardest balance: let the agent do the work, but keep the user in charge
